SignalForge: The B2B Signal-to-Meeting Agent

The evaluated signal-to-meeting layer: detects real buying signals, researches accounts with grounded facts, drafts personalized first-touch emails — and only lets them through an HHH eval gate, with a human approving every send.

Role: AI PM — Amin

01The Thesis

The Problem

20–40 min

Per account: detecting the signal, researching, finding the contact, and writing a genuinely personalized email. At 50+ target accounts, coverage collapses. Existing AI-SDR tools solve volume, not trust — they hallucinate funding rounds and send generic drafts, burning domain reputation and the prospect's patience.

Strategic Pivot

The Gate Is The Differentiator

Every AI-SDR tool generates volume; almost none put an eval gate between the model and the prospect's inbox. SignalForge gates every draft on Helpful / Honest / Harmless — with an asymmetric Honest floor — and a human approves every send. No auto-send, ever. That's a safety boundary, not a limitation.

03Architecture

schema

Harness + Tools + Memory + Gate

DETECT (hiring/funding/news) → ENRICH → RESEARCH (grounded facts + explicit null path) → DRAFT (≤90 words, 4 sentences) → HHH EVAL GATE → HUMAN APPROVE QUEUE

An hourly cron drives the loop. The researcher may only return facts with an evidence field — if it can't find a specific hook, it returns null instead of inventing one. Drafts that fail the gate regenerate (max 2) or get flagged; approved outcomes feed memory.

The Methodology

HHH adapted to outbound: Helpful = the hook is specific to THIS account. Honest = every claim traces to stored research evidence (groundedness). Harmless = compliant and non-creepy — no scraped personal data, no false urgency, no manipulative framing.

Test the tester: The golden set includes deliberately bad drafts — a hallucinated funding round, a generic "I love what you're doing at {company}", a creepy "I saw you moved to Bangalore." The LLM-judge is validated against human labels until it catches every seeded failure; an unvalidated judge is just a vibe.

The gate formula: overall = min(honest, avg(helpful, harmless)) — and Honest ≤ 3 is an automatic reject regardless of other scores. Regression re-runs the golden set on every prompt change, in CI, with prompts versioned in git.

05PM Judgment

The Hard Decision

The Asymmetric Honest Floor

Drafts enter the queue only at Honest ≥ 4 and overall ≥ 3.8; below that, one regeneration, then a human flag. The Honest floor is asymmetric because a hallucinated claim costs domain reputation, while a bland draft costs only an ignore. The rejected alternative — a single blended threshold — would let a charming hook average away a dishonest claim.

The Honest Scope Call

When NOT To Use This Agent

For a 20-account, high-ACV ABM motion, a human researcher still wins: the list is small enough for depth, and the cost of one bad touch is huge. SignalForge earns its keep at 50+ accounts, where coverage — not depth — is the binding constraint. Writing down where the product shouldn't be used is the judgment being sold.

The Trust Boundary

AI Owns

Signal detection & normalization, web research into structured facts, first-draft writing, judge scoring.

Human Owns

Choosing the ICP and which signals matter, final approval + any edit, auditing the judge on a sample.

Never Automated

Sending. Ever. The unsafe loop is auto-send — reputation risk is asymmetric and irreversible.

Cost per draft 20–40 min of human time Fractions of a cent — the real cost is review time

The Routing Rationale

Research and drafting are patterned tasks: a cheap-tier model held eval scores within points of the frontier model at ~1/15th the cost. Escalation to the frontier model happens only when the eval gate rejects twice — the eval, not the vibe, decides the spend.

07Retrospective

Review Fatigue Is Real

If humans approve 100% of drafts instantly, the gate is theater. Approval rate is tracked over time: climbing approval means the agent is learning; instant rubber-stamping means the review process needs redesigning, not celebrating.

Honesty About Coverage

Free-tier enrichment gets ~50–70% verified emails, not 95% — and that real number is published, with LinkedIn manual touch as the fallback for misses. Scrapers rot, so detectors log failures instead of crashing the run. Stating limits reads as judgment, not weakness.
