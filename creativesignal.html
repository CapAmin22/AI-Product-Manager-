CreativeSignal: D2C Creative Pipeline + Signal-Health Monitor

Evaluated creative volume + clean signal — the two levers that matter in the Andromeda era. Every variant must pass a pre-flight brand/compliance gate before it can reach a campaign, and a signal-health dashboard flags conversion-tracking degradation within a day.

Role: AI PM — Amin

01The Thesis

The Problem

15–20

Diverse creatives per campaign cycle is what algorithmic delivery now demands — but generation tools produce volume without judgment: off-brand tone, non-compliant claims, near-duplicate angles. Meanwhile conversion tracking silently degrades to iOS ATT, ITP, and ad-blockers, so the algorithm learns from corrupted data and CPMs creep up.

Strategic Pivot

Two Tracks, One System

The algorithm finds the audience; the job is to feed it evaluated diversity and clean signal. CreativeSignal ships both: a gated generation loop where nothing off-brand or non-compliant reaches spend, and a daily signal-health monitor that catches degradation from a dashboard — not from a CPM spike three weeks later.

The Gated Loop + Parallel Monitor

BRIEF → PLAN → GENERATE → CLASSIFY → DEDUP → PRE-FLIGHT GATE (compliance · brand · quality) → REVIEW → EXPORT ‖ SIGNAL-HEALTH MONITOR (daily cron)

Layered gating: deterministic compliance rules hard-block violations with the rule named; a brand-voice judge scores against a written rubric; embedding dedup flags any pair above 0.85 cosine similarity. The monitor tracks EMQ proxy, dedup rate, pixel-server gap, and volume anomalies — green / amber / red.

The Goldilocks Gate Pass-Rate Band

The Methodology

Three-layer truth: Compliance is deterministic ground truth (precision/recall against 15 seeded violations — a "guaranteed results" claim, off-brand slang, fake scarcity). Brand voice is judged against a written rubric, validated against human labels. Quality is judged and correlated with any outcome data available.

The rubric is the product: A 1-page brand rubric with do/don't examples, banned words, and claim rules — validated with a second human labeler and Cohen's kappa ≥ 0.6. If two humans can't agree from the rubric, no judge can enforce it.

The signal-monitor eval: A synthetic 60-day event log with a deliberate degradation injected on day 45 (email match-key coverage drops 90% → 40%). The monitor must fire amber→red within one check cycle and explain which metric moved — the demo moment.

05PM Judgment

The Hard Decision

The Brand-Voice Floor: ≥4, Not ≥3

At ≥3 the gate passes ~90% of variants but lets mediocre-fit copy through; at ≥4 it passes ~68% and regeneration costs rise ~20%. Because an off-brand impression damages brand equity while a blocked variant costs only tokens, the floor is high — asymmetric costs demand asymmetric thresholds.

The Rejected Alternative

One Blended Score

A single mega-prompt scoring compliance + brand + quality at once was rejected twice over: you can't tune or trust what you can't decompose, and compliance failures must be absolute — never averaged away by a charming hook. Compliance fails are also never regenerated blindly: they're information about the generator prompt, not a per-variant patch.

The Trust Boundary

AI Owns

Generating diverse variants at volume, classifying archetype/funnel, scoring against the written rubric, flagging signal anomalies.

Human Owns

The brief, the archetype taxonomy, the taste call on final selection, final brand-safety & legal sign-off, auditing the judge monthly.

Never Automated

Publishing to platforms or pausing spend. Variants export as a reviewed CSV — no spend is ever touched.

The Kill / Scale Call

A batch of 20 evaluated variants costs ~$X. If evaluated diversity lifts hook-rate even modestly on a $Z monthly spend, payback is N batches. Cost is not the constraint — trust is. That's why the gate exists.

07Retrospective

The Goldilocks Gate

A 100% pass-rate means the gate is theater; 20% means the generator or rubric is broken. The healthy signature is 60–80% pass with a climbing human-approval rate — presented side by side on the dashboard so the gate's honesty is always visible.

Diversity Must Be Measured

Judging diversity by eyeball misses mode collapse — 20 near-identical paraphrases look "different enough" until you embed them. The similarity check makes "genuinely different" measurable, and the classifier disagreeing with the intended archetype became a diversity failure worth logging.
