HookLens: The Ad-Creative Classifier + Eval Harness

A structured-output service that classifies & scores marketing hooks — wrapped in a real evaluation harness with a labeled golden set, confidence calibration, and an explicit human-in-the-loop trust threshold.

Role: AI PM — Amin

01The Thesis

The Problem

~95%

Of AI-PM candidates ship classifiers with no eval harness. Performance teams generate hundreds of hooks, but nobody can say — at scale — what kind each one is, which funnel stage it serves, or whether the label can be trusted. Manual tagging doesn't scale; gut-feel tagging is inconsistent.

Strategic Pivot

Measured, Not Demoed

A typed classifier is a commodity. The product is the evidence around it: a hand-labeled golden set, macro-F1 with a confusion matrix, confidence calibration, and a written trust boundary that says exactly when a human must stay in the loop. I don't ship AI I haven't measured.

The Methodology

Golden set over volume: ~100 real hooks pulled from the Meta Ad Library, hand-labeled with a written rubric — deliberately over-sampling the hard, ambiguous cases (hooks that are both curiosity AND FOMO), because that's where the model breaks and the eval earns its keep.

Split ground truth from judgment: Archetype and funnel stage are graded against my labels (F1 + confusion matrix). Brand-voice is subjective, so it's scored by a separate rubric-driven LLM-as-judge — validated against my human scores on a sample. An unvalidated judge is just a vibe.

Calibration is the ship rule: If answers marked "0.9 confident" aren't right ~90% of the time, the confidence number is lying and can't drive automation. Accuracy is banded by confidence, and the auto-accept threshold sits where the error rate stays under 5%.

05PM Judgment

The Hard Decision

Setting the Auto-Accept Threshold

At ≥0.7 confidence I'd automate ~85% of volume but ship ~12% mislabels; at ≥0.85 I'd cover only ~55% but hold errors under 5%. Because the downstream use is a spend decision, I chose ≥0.85 and routed the rest to review — I'd rather under-automate than feed a wrong label into a budget call.

The Rejected Alternative

One Threshold For Everything

A single fixed threshold across all fields ignored that brand-voice scoring is noisier than archetype classification — so thresholds are set per-field instead. Same call on single-label vs. multi-label: forced a primary label (what teams actually want), documented the ambiguity rather than hiding it.

The Trust Boundary

AI Owns

First-pass archetype / angle / funnel classification at scale; consistent triage a human can act on.

Human Owns

Final brand-safety sign-off, legal / claims review, and any "kill this campaign" spend decision.

The Rule

Below the confidence threshold → route to a human. Above → auto-accept, spot-checked.

06Economics

Classification (archetype, funnel) Cheap, temperature 0 Fractions of a cent per hook — the eval proves the cheap model holds up
Brand-voice judging Cheap, rubric-driven Schema constrains output to exactly the fields needed — no wasted tokens
Whole-library scoring Batch API Offline jobs run at ~half price

The Kill / Scale Call

A cheap model at cents per thousand hooks is defensible; the frontier model's marginal F1 gain didn't justify ~10× the cost. Model routing is the decision — the eval harness is what makes it defensible instead of a guess.

07Retrospective

Confidence Lies

Models over-trust their own confidence on ambiguous hooks — which is exactly why calibration, not raw confidence scores, drives the ship rule. The threshold is a business decision tied to the cost of a wrong label, not a technical default.

The Golden Set Is The Asset

The single most valuable artifact isn't the code — it's the labeled golden set. It encodes taste and a point of view on creative, and it's what turns every future prompt change or model swap from a guess into a measured before/after.
